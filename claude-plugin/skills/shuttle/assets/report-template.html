§ Standing findings

What is true, and how firmly.

Cut-sky Gaussian covariance under-covers, worst at low-z shear

Against 200 seeds the NaMaster Gaussian diagonal is low by 20–35% in the first two tomographic bins and within 5% above z ≈ 0.8. Every σ in this report therefore uses the ensemble. Numbers: results/cov_compare.json.

Exact-truth sampling recovers every probe inside the window

Catalog-level recovery is within 1σ of input across all auto- and cross-spectra for 40 < ℓ < 700. Outside the window the integral constraint biases the lowest bandpower low by ~8% — expected, and the reason the window starts at ℓ = 40. one field only

The solver's convergence flag carries no information

It zero-divides on exact-zero cross spectra, so its warnings fire on healthy runs. We audit per-multipole residuals instead; those are exact to < 3 × 10⁻⁶.
